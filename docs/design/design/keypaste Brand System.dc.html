Logo

The mark is a lowercase k made from two parts: the stem is a text cursor and the arm is an insert bracket. Together they mean "paste here". The wordmark is always lowercase, set in Instrument Sans Semibold at −4.5% tracking.

07

Voice

Precise and plain. Write like a good CLI's help text: say who wants what, for how long, and what happens next. Use sentence case, "you" for the person, the agent's real name for the agent, and no exclamation marks.

name the actor and the objectclaude-code wants DATABASE_URLAn application is requesting access to a credential
always give the durationAllow for 1 hourTrust this app
errors say what to do nextVault is locked. Touch your YubiKey to continue.Oops! Something went wrong.
