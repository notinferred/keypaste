## Limits and follow-ups

No one has seen Increase Contrast or a Windows contrast theme switch the app: the tests raise the preference through Avalonia's settings notification, and `libAvaloniaNative` and `Avalonia.Win32` were only read to hold the code that reports it. A Windows contrast theme with its own colours, such as Aquatic, gets keypaste's high-contrast palette, not those colours. Linux's preference was not checked.

Disabled text and the hairlines of cards, tables and dividers stay under WCAG's ratios outside high contrast, as WCAG allows. Scrollbar thumbs, the segmented control's track and a list's resize line stay on quiet lines, under 3:1, though a person operates them. The 20 by 20 targets were measured on the sidebar folds, the clear-scope button and the chip-remove style, not on every button. `site/public/brand.css` is unchanged.

Drawn without a control edge, as the founder left them because each reads as text by design: `DestinationField` is transparent at rest and reads as text until hovered; `ComboBox.project` has no box. `ToggleSwitchStrokeOff` and `ToggleSwitchStrokeOffPointerOver` are brushes nothing draws, because `Inputs.axaml` replaces the switch's template, so a switch's edge does not change on hover; only its knob does. Frames draw neither hover states nor, on the screens looked at, a date picker, so those rest on the resource tests.
