### 2026-11-05: the icon rail

The founder directed this on 2026-11-05 as an amendment to Q.10 and Q.18, so it has no STEPS row: under 840 design pixels the sidebar collapses to a rail about 56 wide instead of going away. It keeps the four places, with tooltips and each place's name for a screen reader; the group tree and projects open from it in Q.10's overlay; the title bar's button goes while the rail shows, its job taken over; View › Hide sidebar still hides everything, with the way back said and reachable. Nothing else changes at 840 and wider, except a menu item that is off there.

Settled in the amendment, all reversible:

- **The rail is one list.** Items, Agents, Trash and Settings are one `ListBox`, `RailNav`, so Up, Down, Home and End walk all four, which the wide sidebar's two lists do not. Its `RailPanel` is a `StackPanel` that lays the last two rows at the foot, where people learned them; a list's arrow keys walk only a navigable panel, so a `Grid` or `DockPanel` would have lost them. Rows are 40 by 40 with icons at 20, as DESIGN asks; the wide sidebar's rows draw theirs at 16 and 14, which this leaves alone. There is no mark on the rail: DESIGN's wordmark floor is 88 wide.
- **Selection** is the sidebar's own: the active ground, no amber, the icon in the text's colour. It follows the place the screen is or is under, so a group or a project stays on Items and Advanced on Settings. Pressing Items shows every item, as the sidebar's row does, from another place and from a group or a project under Items, where the list hears no change of selection and a tap on the row does it; `SelectedRailPlace` asks about a draft through the same path, and Cancel puts the highlight back. Agents under History stays put, as the sidebar's row does.
- **Agents' state.** The brief describes an amber waiting signal; the sidebar's row has none. Its dot is green while answering, red while refusing and grey otherwise, and waiting shows only in its tooltip and its count, so the rail draws that same dot on the icon and adds no amber, which would be a second one beside New. The word, which has no room, goes in the row's accessible name, `Agents, live`, `Agents, live, 2 waiting` or `Agents, refusing`, and the whole of what the row says, such as "Answering agents · 2 waiting · 1 connected", in its tooltip and help text. Counts drop from the rail.
- **Opening the overlay** is a button of its own, `RailGroups`, first on the rail with Q.18's panel icon, and not a press on Items, which goes to Items. DESIGN's rule that every command is in the menus adds View › Show groups and projects, which is off at 840 and wider and with no chord: ⌃⌘S and Ctrl+B stay Hide sidebar at every width. The overlay covers the rail, and the rail leaves the tree while it does, so no place is announced twice. The overlay gives the keyboard to the chosen row, in the footer's list on Trash, Settings and the screens under Settings. Escape hands it back to `RailGroups`, and so does narrowing with the keyboard in the sidebar; widening with it on the rail hands it to the sidebar's chosen row. - **Hide sidebar is now the kept choice at every width.** Under 840 it hides the rail and gives the content its 56; from the overlay it closes that too, rather than asking which was meant, since the menu says Hide sidebar. It is kept across a lock and a change of width, as it was at a wide width.
- **Saying how it comes back.** `ShowSidebar` shows only while the person has hidden the sidebar. Under 840 it carries the words Show sidebar beside its icon, and its tooltip and help text say "The sidebar is hidden. Show it here, from the View menu or with ⌃⌘S." or Ctrl+B; at 840 and wider it stays Q.18's icon with "Show sidebar" and no help text. It is a label on the screen rather than a toast, so someone who opens the window hidden tomorrow still reads it. Bringing the rail back from it, by a press, the chord or the menu, gives the keyboard to the rail's chosen place. At 840 and wider the button's focus falls away as it did, which this leaves alone.
- **The content at 664.** At 840 the content is already 608 wide beside the 232 sidebar, with more columns, so 664 is not a new minimum for any screen. The renderer draws every place and screen under one at 720 by 520. Two things that had been cut gave way under the rail, whose class `narrow-window` on `BodyColumns` their screens' styles read.
