## Limits and follow-ups

- **Unsigned and unnotarized.** Gatekeeper is expected to block a downloaded copy; 3.5b signs and notarizes it.
- **No CLI in the image** until G.5.
- **Not installed.** Nothing dragged the app to `/Applications` or opened it from Finder, and the selftest renders no window; 4.7e installs the DMG and R.1a exercises it.
- **One candidate per RID.** `verify-desktop-candidate.sh` accepts only the DMG for `osx-arm64`, so offering both declared packages fails at `release.yml`'s staging, and offering only the DMG fails `require-release-assets.sh --publishable`. 4.7c2's row now names the choice: stop declaring the zip, or accept each declared `osx-arm64` kind.
