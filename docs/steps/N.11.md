# N.11 — Approve a terminal edit in the unlocked app

Completed 2026-10-19 on `main` at `57e03ca`, source only: ci 39280980932 and app 39280980928. The step was built directly on `main` in several commits, each pushed and run; this record and the STEPS and ROADMAP edits follow in one more.

## Amendments

The founder selected N.11 on 2026-10-19, with removing T2 from STEPS once N.11 left it empty, and approved a plan that settled what the row left open (D-0464):

- **The verbs.** Every verb that edits items is routed, not only the row's `set`, `add`, `rm` and env writers: `add`, `set` (with `--field`), `rm`, `rotate`, `field rm`, `env set`, `env rm`, `env pull` and `env tag`/`untag`. `access`, `import`, `token` and `share` stay refused.
- **Both holders.** The app asks in its prompt window and `keypaste agent` in its terminal, where only `y` allows.
- **Piped values.** They are read and sent, as the founder chose over refusing piped input. A pipe holding a line more than the values the verb reads is refused before anything is sent, so an old script that pipes the master password first sends nothing.
- **Delivery.** The founder directed working directly on `main`, without a task branch or `scripts/dev.sh`. Each push's own `ci` and `app` runs were the evidence. The plan's separate commit for the window and the terminal prompt, unwired, was folded into the one that wires them.
- **The plan.** ROADMAP moves N.11 into 0.5.0, by the founder's direction that 0.5.0 is what is being built, and STEPS loses its T2 section. T2 stays a track in PRODUCT and in ROADMAP's order.

## What changed

- **`Core/Editing/`.** Each verb's vault work, the checks, the questions it asks from a preview, and the write, moved from the ten CLI verbs into `TerminalEdits.Check` and `Apply`, which the verb runs on a vault it opens and the holder runs on its own. Local output and exit codes are unchanged. `Vault.Checkpoint` and `Restore` take back an edit whose save failed.
- **The pipe.** One new `edit` kind carries a `TerminalEdit`: the values it writes, or a recipe the holder generates from, never a master password. A reply carries what was done by name only.
- **The holder.**
  - `SessionEditor` validates the edit, refuses it while the open vault differs from its file, and checks it before asking.
  - It asks under the cooldown every request for more from a terminal or an agent shares, then writes within the unlock it was asked under, refuses a change other than the one shown, and takes back a failed save.
  - `SessionAuthority.EditAsync` admits and records each edit on a `terminal_edit` line.
  - The app's `TerminalEditWindow`, Agents' waiting list, History's wording and a refresh of Items or Projects show it.
  - `keypaste agent` asks in its terminal.
- **The CLI.** `VaultSession.OpenHeld` takes an `OwnerRoute`. When the app or `keypaste agent` holds the vault, `OwnerEdit` attaches, reads the verb's values, refuses a pipe holding more, and sends the edit. With nothing answering, it refuses as before.
