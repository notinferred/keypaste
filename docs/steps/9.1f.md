Three choices the brief leaves open were made here and are D-0590 to D-0592, and the founder accepted all three on 2026-10-28: the entry point is renamed Import from another app; the choice for taken names applies to names the vault already has, as `keypaste import --skip-existing` does, while an item the export holds twice is always kept beside the other, since skipping it would leave out an item the vault does not hold; and the app deletes nothing. ## Limits and follow-ups

- **The app does not delete the export.** It says to and names the path. Import .env's confirmed delete, bound to the bytes it read through `SourceSnapshot`, could be offered here too; that changes SECURITY's plaintext guarantee and is the founder's to decide.
- **One choice for every name the vault already has.** The CLI's `--skip-existing` makes the same choice; a per-item choice is not offered.
- **The dialog does not watch for edits while it is open.** A change made to the open vault between the preview and Import, such as a terminal's edit the app saves, is caught by `Commit`'s check, and the preview is then laid out again.
- **The desktop prototype still draws the New menu's entry as Import KeePass database**; the design handoff names Import from another app, which the founder accepted, and the export dialog.
- **Of the formats keypaste reads, only Bitwarden's has an encrypted form**; the others are plaintext, and a file no reader recognizes is refused as no export keypaste reads.
- The dialog is checked in headless tests only, not observed in an installed app.
