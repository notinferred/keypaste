## What changed

- **The operation** (D-0543, D-0621). `Vault.MergeAnotherSave` reads the file's bytes once per attempt, digests them, refuses them from their header below KDBX 4, and parses them, not the file again, with the key the unlock holds, a hardware key asked again (`KeePassInterop.Reopen(path, bytes, key)`, `KdbxHeader.Of`). It hands that copy to 1.4b1's `Vault.MergeInto`. A merged copy that took nothing from the open copy is returned unsaved; any other is saved under the stale-write check against that digest, keeping the file it replaces whatever the backup floor, and a save landing first starts the merge again, at most three times. Every refusal and failure disposes the merged copy, leaving the open copy and the file as they were; the result, `VaultMergeSave` in `VaultMerge.cs`, names the outcome, the copy, the report and the backup kept.
- **The owner** (D-0542, D-0545, D-0622). `SessionAuthority.Merge` withdraws what waits on the live session as a merge, ending every grant as a reload's withdrawal does, runs the operation, writes a saved merge's line and puts the merged copy in place through the owner's swap while the same lifetime is live. Agents are refused as `vault-changed` until then because the open copy no longer matches its file (D-0317).
- **The withdrawal's cause** (D-0620). `SessionLifetime.Withdraw(SessionWithdrawal)` keeps whether a reload or a merge withdrew what was waiting, and `SessionAuthority`, `SessionEnvResolver` and `SessionEditor` word a withdrawn request by it.
- **The audit line** (D-0619). The tool `vault_merge` and the method `merged` join the vocabulary, built by `RequestRecords.Merge`; Last used skips the line as it skips a terminal's edit.

## Limits and follow-ups

- **No front end merges yet.** 1.4b3 and 1.4b4 call `SessionAuthority.Merge` under their edit gates, swap the copy, lock the app with `ReloadRefused` on `KeyRefused`, show the notice and offer Reload after a refusal.
- **Agent-facing and guide text still speaks only of reloads.** The `vault-changed` refusal an agent reads (`ToolText.VaultChanged`) asks the person to reload, and the audit method tables in [mcp-setup](../mcp-setup.md) and [policy](../policy.md) do not list `merged`; both become incomplete once an owner merges, so 1.4b3 and 1.4b4 rewrite them.
- **A refused save attempt still keeps its backup.** The backup is taken in the save gate before the write, so a merged copy's attempt refused after it, by another save, leaves a copy of the bytes it found; three such attempts can keep three copies and push older ones past the five retained.
