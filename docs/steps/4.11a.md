## What changed

- **Report and scrubber.** `CrashScrubber` makes every `CrashReport`, through `Crash`, `UnobservedTask`, `Unexpected` (with a site's constant name) and `EndedUnexpectedly` (no exception), from an exception and a `CrashContext`: when, the uptime and the screen the app names. It reads the exception's type, HResult and frames, through `new StackTrace(exception, fNeedFileInfo: false)` and `DiagnosticMethodInfo`, the way that stays trim-safe under the analyzers every project runs, then four inner exceptions breadth first, an aggregate's children included. Each name must be an ASCII code identifier of at most 256 characters from an allowed assembly, else it becomes `[unnamed]`, `[external]` or `[dynamic]`; a generic type's arguments, which are assembly-qualified, are cut off, and an exception type from outside the allowlist is reported as its nearest allowed base. A report keeps 48 frames and 16 of each inner exception, counts the rest, and drops frames from the bottom until it is at most 16 KiB. Platform facts are numbers and fixed words only: `windows`, `macos` or `linux`, the process's architecture, the OS and runtime versions, and the uptime as `<1m`, `<10m`, `<1h`, `<1d` or `>1d`. The JSON is compact, in one field order, and read back only when it is byte for byte what the scrubber would write.
- **Outbox.** `CrashOutbox` keeps each distinct report as one owner-only file named by the SHA-256 of its bytes with the count left out, so an identical report merges into it; at most 20 reports and 256 KiB, the oldest written dropped first, and none from more than 30 days ago. Any `*.json` file there that does not read back as a scrubbed report under its own name is deleted. `Remove` leaves a report an identical failure was merged into after it was read, and `Clear`, for the switch turned off, deletes the folder. `KeypasteHome.CrashReportsPath` names `~/.keypaste/crash-reports/`. No call writes anything until a report is added, and nothing in IO throws out of it.
- **Sender.** `CrashSender` takes an `HttpMessageHandler`, so tests use a fake, and an endpoint, refusing any but keypaste.com and a loopback origin; `TryResolveEndpoint` reads `KEYPASTE_CRASH_URL` the way `ShareEndpoint` reads its variable, refusing anything but a loopback origin. `CreateTransport` follows no redirect, keeps no cookie and adds no trace header. `RunAsync` waits 30 seconds, then sends a pass every hour; a pass sends up to ten reports, oldest first, each in 10 seconds, and stops at the first not taken. Nothing in Core starts it.
- **Vector.** `site/test/crash-vector.json` holds three reports, a crash with an inner exception and an external frame, a merged unexpected exception, and an app that ended with no hook, as their exact bytes, for 4.11b's route tests to accept.
