## Ideas

Keep ideas to one line and remove shipped or rejected rows. Promotion to STEPS requires a falsifiable Verify line. Financial figures stay outside git.

Statuses are open (undecided), parked (deferred with a reason), rejected, promoted (a STEPS task) and shipped.

### UI direction

Steps 4.1 and 4.2 adopted a calm, precise interface with whitespace, a muted palette, one accent, Inter, subtle depth and full dark-mode support. Reserve warnings for exceptional actions; avoid terminal styling, repeated padlock icons and dense tree tables. The approval dialog establishes the identity by showing who requests what and why.
